## Key finding: how `best_z` works
`best_z` in `best_focus.tsv` is a **0-based position in the sorted list of slices that `all_files` lists** for that image and channel. It is not the z number in the filename. They are the same unless a stack has gaps or does not start at z0, which only Study 24 has (79 of 92 stacks).

- First reading (wrong): Study 24 had 27 best-focus files "missing". Reality: they were positions, e.g. best_z = 2 on slices `[0, 3, 4, 7, 8]` means z4.
- Evidence: position reading matches 92/92 Study 24 stacks, label reading only 28%.
- Earlier drop (12) and skip (24) counts were therefore wrong. Corrected below.
